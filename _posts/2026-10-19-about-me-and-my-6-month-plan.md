## Who I am

I'm an aspiring cyber security analyst. I recently completed the SANS Applied Cybersecurity (ACS) program and hold four GIAC certifications: GFACT, GSEC, GCIH, and GCFA. I'm now moving from training into my first full-time cybersecurity role.

## Where I'm headed

My goal is a full-time, remote-friendly role in incident response, DFIR, or threat hunting. Over the next six months I want to turn my training into hands-on proof and land that first job.

## My 6-month plan

**Months 1-2: Build the lab and sharpen the fundamentals.** I'll set up a home lab for memory, disk, and timeline forensics. I'll practice on TryHackMe and Hack The Box. I'll also map real threat reports (CISA, Mandiant, CrowdStrike) to MITRE ATT&CK techniques, so I can talk about adversary behavior with confidence.

**Months 3-4: Build a public portfolio.** I'll publish at least three investigation write-ups here. Each will walk through a case from initial alert to findings, using public datasets and lab scenarios. I'll also put my scripts and detection notes on GitHub. In parallel, I'll update my LinkedIn and resume and start connecting with people in the DFIR community.

**Months 5-6: Apply and interview.** I'll target incident response analyst, DFIR analyst, threat hunting, and SOC roles. That includes healthcare organizations, managed security providers, and consulting firms. I'll tailor each application to my write-ups and do mock interviews on incident scenarios.

## How I'll track progress

At the end of each month I'll post a short update covering what I finished, what I learned, and what I'm adjusting.

## Follow along

If you work in DFIR, are hiring, or are on a similar path, I'd love to connect. Check back for monthly updates and the investigation write-ups.
